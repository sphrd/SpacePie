SpacePie

The fastest free disk space analyzer and cleaner for Windows and macOS.

Download for Windows Download for macOS Source code on GitHub

Free and open source (MIT licence). One small app, nothing to install. Windows 10 and 11; macOS 11 or later on Apple silicon and Intel.

See where your disk space went

SpacePie starts analyzing your system drive the moment it opens. Find the folders and files that take the most space, big, old and duplicate files, and what every installed app really takes, then free the space: to the Recycle Bin / Trash or permanently.

A whole drive in seconds

As administrator on Windows it reads the NTFS master file table directly: over a million files in about ten seconds. Otherwise, and on macOS, it scans folders in parallel.

Pie chart or treemap

The chart follows the list you are looking at, with subfolders in outer rings or nested rectangles. Click a part to open it.

Cleanup suggestions

Caches, temporary files, old installers, build folders and more, with how much each frees. Safe items start checked; items to review first start unchecked.

Uninstall apps completely

Every installed app with its real size; expand it to see its settings, caches and other files, and remove them together.

Duplicate files

Find identical files by content and free the extra copies, or replace them with links so every file stays where it is.

History and search

Compare any two scans to see what grew, and search everything scanned by name and age.

Instant restart

Scans are saved when you close the app and brought up to date from the change journal (Windows) or the event history (macOS) the next time.

Private and checked

No account, no telemetry. Totals are checked against the operating system's own tools on every build.
